## 📜Background
E-wallets are replacing cash for everyday transactions. However, small businesses and street vendors cannot stop to unlock their phone and check every transaction notification as they are busy.
While many e-wallet or providers offer SoundBox, but this often come with:
* **One-Time Payment around 100 to 300 and/or Monthly subscription fees**
* **Locked ecosystems (only works with one specific bank)**
* **Electronic waste (another device to charge and maintain)**

So this E-Wallet Soundbox Application temporarily turning any smartphone into a dedicated soundbox. It allows vendors to hear and verify every transaction instantly without touching their phone. It’s cost-effective and eco-friendly.

## 🔒Permissions Required

To function correctly, esBox requires the following:
*   **Notification Access:** To read incoming payment alerts.
*   **Battery Optimization Bypass:** To ensure the app stays active in the background.

# ❗Privacy & Security
* This app **doesn't required internet permission**
* **only reads notifications from TnG ewallet**. It ignores all other personal notifications.
* It **only extracts the amount and currency from the notification text**. No names, account numbers, or other sensitive details are stored or processed.
